Isomorphic Labs opened its Lausanne, Switzerland office in May 2023 and later relocated to the Flon innovation hub. The interdisciplinary team of data engineers, ML researchers, computational biologists, and software engineers collaborates on drug discovery projects, with the company planning to expand its scientific disciplines there while maintaining operations across London, Lausanne, and Cambridge, Massachusetts.

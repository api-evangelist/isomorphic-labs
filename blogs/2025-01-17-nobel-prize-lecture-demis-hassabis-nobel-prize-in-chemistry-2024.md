Demis Hassabis, CEO of Isomorphic Labs, received the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for developing AlphaFold 2, which predicts protein structures using AI. He delivered his Nobel lecture, "Accelerating scientific discovery with AI," on December 8, 2024 at Stockholm University, describing how the breakthrough inspired the company's founding in 2021.

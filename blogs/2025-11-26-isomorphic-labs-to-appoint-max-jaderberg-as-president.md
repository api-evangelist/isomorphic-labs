Isomorphic Labs announced that Max Jaderberg will become President effective January 1, 2026, moving from his role as Chief AI Officer. Jaderberg, a founding team member, has led development of the company's AI drug design engine, while current President Colin Murdoch steps down after leading its early growth phase.
